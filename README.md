### 👩‍💻 About me

I have a background in **Applied Mathematics for Economics and Management** at ISEG - Lisbon School of Economics and Management and I'm currently a 2nd year master's student in **Data Science & Advanced Analytics** at Nova IMS. I'm interested in turning data into clear, well-grounded answers, combining solid statistical foundations with practical data skills.

I care about reproducible work: clean code, organised repositories and analyses that someone else can pick up and run.

### 📊 What I work on

- **Statistics & Econometrics**: regression, inference and statistical modelling
- **Data analysis**: cleaning, exploring and visualising real-world data
- **Data science**: predictive models and machine learning workflows
- **Optimisation**: mathematical programming and optimisation methods for decision problems
